Belgian procedure. Part-time employees under PC 200 are entitled to the end-of-year bonus,
often called the 13th month. The bonus is calculated pro rata: a part-time employee working
50% receives 50% of the full-time end-of-year bonus. The reference period runs from 1 January
to 31 December. Payment happens with the December payroll.
