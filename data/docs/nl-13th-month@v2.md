Dutch procedure (replaces the 2025 note). Dutch law has no statutory 13th month or end-of-year
bonus. Whether the customer's part-time employees are entitled depends on the applicable CAO or
the employment contract. If a CAO grants an end-of-year bonus, part-time employees receive it
pro rata to their working time under the equal treatment rule (Wet onderscheid arbeidsduur).
Steps: 1) identify the CAO, 2) check the bonus clause, 3) apply pro rata for part-timers.
